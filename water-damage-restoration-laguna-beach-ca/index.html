Terrain And Season

Hills, Canyons and a Low Downtown: How Water Moves Here

Water problems in Laguna Beach follow the ground. The city's April 2024 Climate Vulnerability Assessment says about a third of residents live in a landslide hazard zone, and that Laguna Canyon Road and Pacific Coast Highway both cross landslide zones. On a slope, rain runs downhill toward garages, lower floors and retaining walls, so tell the dispatcher which level is wet.

The same assessment notes that after the 1993 Laguna Fire, severe landslides followed only mild rain, and a 2005 landslide in Bluebird Canyon forced about 350 homes to evacuate (USGS ties it most likely to heavy rain earlier that year). Clear gutters and yard drains before a storm.

Before We Arrive

Before We Arrive: If Water Is Still Spreading

Six moves that limit damage, none of which needs tools.

Stop the source

Shut the fixture valve or the main water valve. If the leak is on the street side of the meter, call your water district.

Power only if safe

Switch off breakers for wet areas only if the panel is dry and reachable without stepping in water.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, electronics and paperwork upstairs or onto dry furniture.

Keep people out

Children and pets stay clear of water that may be sewage or touch wiring.

Take photos

Photograph the water line, damaged items and the source.

Local Risks

Laguna Beach Risks We Plan Around

City, state and federal sources, each with a practical consequence.

Downtown floodgates

The city assessment says City Code requires floodgates downtown, and sandbags are kept at fire stations, the City Corporate Yard and the Aliso Beach parking lot. Know where your gates are before rain.

Water rising from below

The assessment projects emergent groundwater near Aliso Creek and in low blocks east of Main Beach, bounded by Broadway, 3rd and Mermaid streets. Groundwater can cut storm-drain capacity, so a floor can wet from beneath, and we take moisture readings after pumping.

Flood zones on named roads

The largest flood hazard zones run along Rim Rock Drive and Laguna Canyon Road, and about 98 residents live in flood hazard areas, mostly the 100-year zone. Check the FEMA flood map for your address.

Sewer and heavy rain

In 2019 a corroded relief valve on the city's North Coast Interceptor failed over a rainy holiday weekend and released about 1.7 million gallons of raw sewage, per the Regional Water Board. Sewage water is Category 3 and needs professional cleanup.
