Before You Dial

What To Have Ready When You Call

A few details help the dispatcher move quickly.

The Address

The street address and cross street, and whether it is a house, condo or apartment.

The Source

What is leaking or what failed, and whether the water has been shut off at the main valve.

The Reach

Which rooms and floors are wet, and about how long the water has been there.

Your Insurer

Whether you plan to file a claim. You do not need a claim number to call.
