The Water Problems Most Likely In Tustin

Before We Arrive

Before We Arrive: If Water Is Still Spreading

A few minutes now can save material later.

Stop the source

Close the fixture valve under the sink or toilet, or shut off the main water valve at the house.

Power off to wet areas

Turn off breakers to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and safe to reach. Otherwise stay back and call your utility.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, electronics and books to a dry room, and put foil under furniture legs that must stay.

Keep people and pets out

Do not wade through water that may be contaminated, and keep children and animals away from wet floors.

Take photos

Photograph the source, wet walls and floors, and damaged belongings before moving anything.

Local Risk

Old Town Pipes, Tustin Legacy Systems, And Shared Walls

Housing age and layout shape the damage here.

Old Town structures

Old Town Tustin, centered on Main Street and El Camino Real, has buildings dating to the 1880s. Original plumbing and wood framing hide moisture, so we probe walls and subfloors.

Tustin Legacy is newer

The Tustin Legacy area was reused after MCAS Tustin closed in 1999, so its homes and buildings are far newer. Expect appliance and fitting failures rather than old pipe.

Most homes are 30 plus years old

The Housing Element says about 64 percent of Tustin's housing stock was more than 30 years old as of 2020. Supply lines, water heaters and drain lines in that stock are common leak points.

Shared-wall buildings

The same Housing Element (ACS 2019) puts about 20 percent of homes in 2 to 9 unit buildings and about 24 percent in buildings of 10 or more. One unit's leak often wets the neighbor below, so tell your landlord or HOA.

Utilities And Permits

Water Providers, Main Breaks And Permits

Tustin Water Services draws groundwater from nine active wells, and emergency interconnections with neighboring suppliers mean a broken main is usually rerouted around. If street water is not from your plumbing, check your bill to learn whether the city or IRWD serves you, then report it there.

Drying alone rarely needs paperwork, but replacing framing, supply lines or wiring can. Ask the Building Division whether your scope needs a permit.

For coverage, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered under a homeowners or renters policy, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer. Our photos and moisture logs support that conversation.

Tustin Water Damage Questions

Is emergency water removal available in Tustin?

Yes. Dispatch is 24/7, nights, weekends and holidays. Call (949) 670-0939 and we offer same-day response when available, without promising an arrival time. Our emergency water removal page explains what the crew does on site.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Tustin?

We cover 92780 and 92782. The 92780 side includes City Hall and Old Town around Main Street and El Camino Real, while 92782 takes in the Tustin Legacy area by the old blimp hangars and the Tustin Ranch area. If your mail ZIP differs, call and tell us the street.

Is my Tustin address on city water or IRWD?

It depends on the address. Tustin Water Services serves the city and part of unincorporated North Tustin, while Irvine Ranch Water District serves other addresses. Your water bill names your provider.

Is flooding a big risk in Tustin?

Less than in most places. The 2019 Hazard Mitigation Plan ranks flood hazard as Low, though the city says low-lying streets can flood quickly. Flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage, so check your policy.

I live in an Old Town house. What should I watch for?

Old Town has buildings from the 1880s, so original plumbing and wood framing are common. Stained plaster, soft baseboards and musty smells can signal a slow leak. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to find wet framing.

Who pays if a leak damages my neighbor's unit?

It depends on the cause, your lease or HOA rules and your policy, so ask your insurer. Photograph both units and notify your landlord or HOA.
