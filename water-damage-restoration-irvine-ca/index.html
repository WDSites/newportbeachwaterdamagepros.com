Irvine is large and spread out, and a burst supply line in a house differs from a leak dropping through a condo ceiling. Here is how losses happen, who runs the utilities, and what we send.

Housing Era

How Irvine Was Built And Where It Leaks

The city calls Irvine a relatively new master-planned community. About 39 percent of homes are single-family detached, 16 percent attached and 44 percent multifamily. Multifamily units grew 158 percent from 2000 to 2020, so many calls involve condos with shared walls and ceilings.

That layout changes how water behaves. A failed dishwasher hose upstairs drips through the ceiling below, then wicks into the shared wall. A two-story house works the same way, with bathroom plumbing above a living room ceiling. Thermal imaging cameras trace the wet path.

The older villages are a different story. The city's 2017 survey of pre-1992 homes covered El Camino Real, Woodbridge, University Park, Walnut Village, Northwood, Westpark I, Rancho San Joaquin, Turtle Rock and University Town Center. Those homes are roughly 30 to 60 years old, an age when supply lines, water heaters and drain lines wear out.

Buildings around the Orange County Great Park are very new, so losses there come more from failed fixtures, appliance hoses and roof or window leaks. Slab leaks, attic leaks and a plugged HVAC condensate line can hit any age of building.

Active Emergency

When Water Is Spreading In Irvine

If water is moving across a floor or dripping from a ceiling right now, treat it as an emergency and call (949) 670-0939. Sewage, a gas smell or water near live electrical equipment is different: get everyone out and call 911 or the utility first, then call us.

Clean supply-line water is Category 1, and it slides toward Category 2 or 3 as it sits in carpet and debris. That is why emergency water removal starts with extraction units.

Before We Arrive

Before We Arrive: If Water Is Still Spreading

Do only what is safe.

Stop the source

Close the fixture shutoff or the main water valve.

Power only if safe

Shut off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and reachable without standing in water.

Lift valuables

Move electronics, papers and small furniture to dry surfaces.

Keep people and pets out

Stay out of standing water that may be contaminated, especially from a drain or toilet.

Take photos

Photograph the source and wet rooms before moving anything. Photos support a claim.

Drainage And Utilities

Drainage, Utilities And Local Risk

These local points affect what you do next.

One district for water and sewer

Irvine Ranch Water District supplies the water and collects the wastewater. A street main break or main-line sewer backup goes to IRWD, so call them while we handle the interior.

Local drains versus county channels

The city owns local storm drains, designed for 25-year flows, and OC Public Works owns the regional flood channels. If a street ponds in a hard storm, photograph water at a garage door for your claim.

Channel corridors and low commercial areas

The city's study of the Irvine Business Complex puts it in the San Diego Creek watershed and notes ponding near channels. Check the FEMA flood map for your address.

Condos, renters and HOAs

Only 44.5 percent of Irvine housing is owner-occupied, so many losses involve renters and landlords. Sort out who carries the claim early.

Common Questions

Irvine Water Damage Questions

Is emergency water removal available in Irvine?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7, nights and weekends included, with same-day response when available. We do not promise an arrival time, but we tell you what to do while you wait. The crew brings extraction units, air movers and moisture meters.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Irvine?

Yes, we cover 92602, 92603, 92604, 92606, 92612, 92614, 92617, 92618, 92620 and 92697. The Civic Center sits in 92606, the Northwood post office is listed under 92602, 92620 is the largest, and 92697 is the UC Irvine campus.

Not sure which ZIP your village uses? Give the dispatcher your street.

Who do I call if the water is coming from the street or sewer?

IRWD supplies the water and runs sewer collection, so a street main break or main-line backup goes to IRWD. Call them for the public side and us for drying inside.

If sewage has backed into the home, stay out of the water and treat it as Category 3.

My upstairs neighbor's pipe leaked into my condo. Who handles the claim?

Stop the water and photograph the damage. Then find out who owns the source (neighbor, landlord or HOA) and ask your own insurer how the claim works. We record moisture readings and affected rooms so the scope is on paper.

Does a repair in Irvine need a permit?

It depends on the scope, so ask. City of Irvine Building & Safety, 1 Civic Center Plaza, 949-724-6000, can say whether your repair needs one. We explain scope and price before work starts.

How fast does mold start after a leak?

Mold needs moisture, and the EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours. A concealed leak in a wall or attic can sit longer.

If mold is visible, ask about mold remediation to the S520 standard, and take health questions to a physician.
